# Local Business OS

An AI-powered operating system for small businesses that helps them manage and improve their digital presence, customer conversations, leads, and growth.

## Problem

Many small businesses have a website, social profiles, customer enquiries, reviews, and business information spread across different places. Owners often do not have the time or technical expertise to understand what is missing, respond consistently, or turn their online presence into a reliable source of business.

Local Business OS is being built to bring these workflows into one practical system.

## Product Vision

The product follows a simple loop:

**Discover → Understand → Find gaps → Build → Publish / Connect → Measure → Improve**

Instead of simply generating a website, the goal is to help a real business continuously improve its digital presence and customer journey.

## Current Prototype

The current development work includes:

- A structured **Business Brain** for storing business information
- Business profile, services, FAQs, service areas and reviews
- Customer conversations and lead records
- Basic intent detection for incoming enquiries
- Controlled responses based on the business's stored information
- Escalation when an enquiry requires human attention
- REST API for the core application
- Dashboard-oriented workflows for business owners
- Owner-to-business relationships
- Mobile-friendly business saving/onboarding flow
- Multi-select business goals
- Capturing requests to build missing website or social presence
- A seeded example business for testing the system

## Why It Matters

The long-term goal is to give small businesses a practical digital layer that they can actually operate themselves.

The project is focused on the gap between:

1. **Having an online presence**, and
2. **Actually using that presence to understand customers, capture opportunities, and improve the business.**

Rather than treating a website as a static brochure, Local Business OS is being developed as an evolving business system.

## Development Status

This project is actively being developed as a working prototype. The current focus is moving from individual features toward an end-to-end workflow that a real small-business owner could use.

Key priorities are:

- Making onboarding fast
- Turning business information into a useful source of truth
- Making customer enquiries actionable
- Identifying gaps in a business's online presence
- Connecting recommendations to actual actions
- Measuring whether those actions improve the business

## Intended Users

The initial target users are small and local businesses such as:

- Home-service businesses
- Interior and renovation businesses
- Local professional services
- Shops and service providers
- Other businesses that depend on local customers and enquiries

## Project Goal

The immediate goal is not to build another generic AI chatbot.

It is to build a system that can be handed to a real business owner and provide measurable value from the first session.
